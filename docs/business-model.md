# Business model — GMB

Independent public-sector market-entry compliance for The Gambia.

- Gambia Public Procurement Authority (GPPA) public procurement --
  Procurement Act 2022 (GPPA's own site names this Act; its detailed
  provisions and supplier registration-requirements checklist sit
  behind a client-side-rendered application this iteration could not
  read around -- see `src/marketentry/facts.cljc`)
- Companies Department (Ministry of Justice) business/company
  registration -- own published fee schedule tiered by declared share
  capital (up to D500,000: D10,000; D500,000-D1,000,000: D15,000;
  D1,000,000-D10,000,000: D20,000; above D10,000,000: D25,000)
- Gambia Revenue Authority (GRA) Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN)
  registration -- established by Act No. 13 of 2004, amended by Act
  No. 10 of 2010
- Gambia Investment & Export Promotion Agency (GIEPA) Act 2015 Special
  Investment Certificate (SIC) gate -- bars an engagement declaring it
  seeks the SIC from proceeding unless its own declared investment
  amount clears the threshold for its own declared investor origin
  ($100,000 domestic / $250,000 foreign)
